About Me

A look into my academic journey, background, and dedication to building reliable software.

Hello! I am Poonam Khare, a Computer Science student enrolled in the integrated B.Tech–M.Tech in Computer Science program at IIPS DAVV, Indore.

My interest in computing started with curiosity about how logical rules translate into interactive applications. Coming from a strong academic background built during my schooling at Kinder Higher Secondary School, Dewas—where I secured 90% in Class 10 and 90.4% in Class 12 (with a 93% PCM aggregate)—I strive to bring rigor, patience, and analytical discipline to every problem I tackle.

In my college coursework and personal practice, I maintain a 8.21 CGPA while actively developing practical programming capabilities. Rather than relying on memorization, I focus on core fundamentals: understanding data structures, writing clean algorithmic code in C++, and progressively expanding into Python, data analysis, and machine learning.

I believe in honest learning through hands-on practice—breaking down LeetCode problems, writing readable solutions, collaborating with peers, and building projects that have genuine utility.
