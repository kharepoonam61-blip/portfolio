# Assets Directory Instructions for Poonam Khare

This directory is set up so you can easily drop in your real photo and certificates without editing complex code.

## 1. Your Portrait Photo
- **File Name:** `portrait.jpg` (or `.png`)
- **Location:** `assets/portrait.jpg`
- **Recommended Ratio:** 4:5 or standard vertical portrait.
- **How it works:** Once you place `portrait.jpg` into this folder, your portfolio hero section will automatically display your real photograph instead of the monogram placeholder frame.

## 2. Your Certificates
Place your original certificates into the `assets/certificates/` folder:
- `assets/certificates/pre-devfest-certificate.png` (or `.jpg`) — Your GDG Cloud Indore Pre-DevFest Workshop certificate.
- `assets/certificates/certificate-2.png` — Your second certificate.

The portfolio will automatically link and display them in the certificate cards and lightbox modal!
